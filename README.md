## Introduction
Climate change is one of the most pressing challenges facing humanity today. Rising greenhouse gas emissions from fossil fuels, deforestation, and industrial processes have led to severe consequences such as rising sea levels, extreme weather events, and threats to ecosystems and public health. This project aims to analyze climate data using advanced tools and statistical models to recommend strategies for limiting global temperature rise to below 2°C by 2100.

---

## Problem Statement
The core challenge is to determine the optimal mix of policies, technological advancements, and behavioral changes required to limit global temperature rise to no more than 2°C by 2100. This goal faces significant challenges, including economic dependence on fossil fuels, population growth, and the need for global cooperation on climate initiatives.

---

## Analysis
The project employs a combination of the **EN-ROADS Climate Solutions Simulator** and statistical models to analyze climate data and predict future trends. Key analyses include:
- Taxing fossil fuels (coal, oil, natural gas).  
- Subsidizing renewable energy.  
- Promoting electrification in transportation.  
- Incorporating carbon dioxide removal technologies. 
Our analysis reduced the projected temperature rise to 2.0°C by 2100 under optimal scenarios.
 

### Linear Regression
- **Objective**: Examine the relationship between coal consumption and global temperature rise.
- **Findings**: Each additional Exajoule of coal consumed increases global temperature by 0.0118°C. Reducing coal usage by 10% could lower temperature rise by 0.118°C.
- **Model Accuracy**: R² value of 0.8608, indicating strong predictive accuracy.

### Probability and Statistics
- **Objective**: Analyze trends and uncertainties in climate data.
- **Findings**: No significant difference between baseline and current scenarios in terms of mean temperatures. However, aggressive interventions have a 75% chance of limiting temperature rise to below 2.5°C.

### Sensitivity Analysis
- **Objective**: Identify the most influential factors affecting climate outcomes.
- **Findings**: Renewable energy adoption is the most significant driver of temperature reduction. Coal consumption is inversely related to temperature rise.

### Decision Analysis
- **Objective**: Evaluate different energy sources based on decision-making criteria.
- **Findings**: Renewable energy is the most favorable option across all criteria, including Maximax, Maximin, and Expected Value.

---

## Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following strategies are recommended to mitigate global temperature rise:
1. **Global Carbon Tax**: Implement a robust carbon tax to reduce reliance on fossil fuels.
2. **Renewable Energy Subsidies**: Prioritize subsidies for renewable energy to accelerate adoption.
3. **Energy Efficiency Improvements**: Enhance energy efficiency in transportation, buildings, and industries.
4. **Transportation Electrification**: Promote the adoption of electric vehicles (EVs) to reduce emissions.
5. **Carbon Dioxide Removal Technologies**: Invest in afforestation and direct air capture technologies.
6. **Addressing Other Greenhouse Gas Sources**: Reduce emissions from agriculture and waste management.

---

## Conclusion
Addressing global warming requires a multifaceted approach that combines innovative policies, advanced technologies, and global cooperation. The EN-ROADS simulator and statistical analyses demonstrate that targeted interventions such as carbon taxation, renewable energy subsidies, and transportation electrification can significantly limit temperature rise. By implementing these strategies, we can mitigate the severe impacts of climate change and ensure a sustainable future.
